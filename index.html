League of Kicks

How the League of Kicks mobile football game handles your information. Türkçe metin aşağıdadır.

Developer: OmniW Effective / Yürürlük: 29 October 2026 Contact: 1omniwai@gmail.com

English

In short: the current version of League of Kicks runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, we show no ads, and we use no analytics or tracking tools.

1. Who we are

League of Kicks ("the game") is developed and published on Google Play by OmniW ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email us at 1omniwai@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you play, the game saves the following only on your device:

  • the player and team names you type in, and your appearance and logo choices;
  • your squad, player attributes, career and league progress, transfer market history, coins and rewards;
  • formation, tactics, daily quest progress and settings (sound, graphics, controls).

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Information we do not collect

The current version does not collect or transmit personal data to our servers. In particular, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or account details;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photos, videos, audio, files or calendar data;
  • the advertising ID or other identifiers used for advertising or tracking.

The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo or storage permissions. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

4. Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes information such as your Google account and installation details under the Google Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. We use these statistics only to fix bugs and improve stability.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Future online features

We plan to add online features such as account sign-in, online matches, an online transfer market, chat and in-app purchases. Before any such feature is switched on, we will update this policy, the information inside the game and the Google Play Data safety section to describe what data is processed and why, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

7. Deleting your data

Because all game data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time:

  • Android: Settings › Apps › League of Kicks › Storage › Clear data, or
  • uninstall the game.

We hold no copy of this data, so nothing remains with us after you delete it. You can also email 1omniwai@gmail.com with any data request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the current version does not send personal data to us, most of these rights can be exercised directly on your device. For any request, contact us at the email address above and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Please keep your device secure, for example with a screen lock.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you inside the game.
